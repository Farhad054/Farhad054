### About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student (graduating Apr 2027) who builds things that actually ship.

Currently the lead developer at **[Mindcrafti](https://mindcrafti.de)** — a bilingual ed-tech platform I architected from scratch (Spring Boot backend, React/TypeScript frontend, Docker, PostgreSQL). I review PRs, own product decisions, and keep the whole thing running.

Before that: interned at a fintech company in Baku (payments platform, Java/Spring Boot), a digital agency in Toronto (React, Next.js, TypeScript), and spent a summer in a McGill robotics lab implementing Q-learning and game-theoretic foraging strategies for multi-agent simulations.

My degree covers both sides. On the hardware side, I've built STM32 firmware with FreeRTOS and co-designed a pipelined RISC-V CPU in VHDL, so I'm comfortable at the application layer and below it.
